WHAT WE CAN PLAN TOGETHER

Site insight, delivered.

Start with the question your team needs to answer. Then define the capture and deliverable around it.

01

Orthomosaic site maps

A stitched overhead view of the jobsite for reviewing access, staging areas, and visible site conditions.

Discuss: coverage area, image resolution, file format, and update schedule.

02

Stockpile volume reports

Image-based volume estimates to support material tracking and discussions with your earthwork team.

Discuss: pile boundaries, base surface, site access, and measurement verification. Accuracy depends on the capture and processing method.

03

Plan & imagery overlays

Compare supplied design drawings with aerial imagery for visual project coordination.

Discuss: drawing format, alignment references, and intended use. These visuals do not replace professional layout or survey verification.

04

Progress documentation

Repeat aerial views to give stakeholders a visual record of visible changes over time.

Discuss: capture frequency, repeat viewpoints, and image organization.

Define the scope first.

Equipment, site conditions, permissions, and required accuracy must be reviewed before any work is agreed. No survey-grade accuracy or licensed surveying service is represented here.
