Scheduling

Send your property location, preferred dates, and listing deadline. I’ll check my NJIT class schedule before confirming a shoot.

Weather requirements

Wind and gusts can affect stability and footage quality. Forecasts, rain, visibility, airspace, and the selected drone’s limits are reviewed ahead of time and on the day. Unsuitable conditions mean discussing a new date before flying.

Looking ahead: construction and mapping

I hope to expand into construction documentation and advanced drone mapping, with surveying-related work as a longer-term goal subject to appropriate qualifications and professional involvement. These are future plans. Licensed surveying and engineering services are not currently offered.
