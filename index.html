WHY ADD AERIAL MEDIA?

Show more of the property.

Compare what each format helps a buyer see.

Exterior details

Ground-level photos show the facade and entrance. They can leave the roof, rear yard, and wider setting outside the frame.

Lot and surroundings

Aerial views add the roof, outdoor space, access, and nearby features in one frame. Useful when the setting is part of the listing’s appeal.

Room-to-room flow

A video connects spaces in sequence, helping buyers understand how the rooms relate before visiting.

Which package suits my listing?

Choose photos for exterior and lot context, video for a short moving overview, or the bundle for both. Choose the home tour when you need every room included. Share your property size and priorities for a recommendation.

Can I request extras?

Yes. Ask for agent branding, extra photos, or coverage of specific features. Extras and any additional fees are confirmed in the quote.

How do scheduling and weather work?

Send preferred dates and your listing deadline. I’ll check class availability, access, airspace, and permissions. Wind, gusts, rain, visibility, and equipment limits are reviewed before flight; unsuitable conditions may require a new date.

What should be ready at the property?

Arrange owner permission and access to the agreed areas. For a home tour, prepare every room and discuss the route and filming method in advance. Indoor FPV depends on a suitable setup and safe access; a single continuous take is not guaranteed.
