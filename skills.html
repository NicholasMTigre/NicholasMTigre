THE PROCESS

From site question
to useful perspective.

A clear brief makes the imagery useful. Every proposed project starts with the intended decision and the information needed to support it.

01 / DEFINE

Tell us about the site.

Share the location, project stage, timeline, and intended use. Identify the areas you need documented and the questions you need answered.

02 / PLAN

Agree on the capture.

Review access, operating requirements, site coordination, weather constraints, and the appropriate deliverable before scheduling.

03 / CAPTURE

Document the conditions.

The proposed capture approach is tailored to the site and agreed scope. Coverage and image quality are reviewed before processing.

04 / REVIEW

Put the output to work.

Review the imagery and accompanying notes together, including any limitations, measurement assumptions, and plans for repeat visits.

A good project starts with a good brief.
