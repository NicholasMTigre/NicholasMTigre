THE PROCESS

Booking & delivery

Agree on the media, timing, and conditions before a shoot is confirmed.

01 / INQUIRE

Property details

Send the location, preferred package, dates, and listing deadline. Mention desired views and whether the video is for a listing or social media.

02 / CONFIRM

Price and availability

I’ll check college availability, airspace, property access, and any required permissions. We’ll agree on price, deliverables, usage, revisions, and expected delivery date before booking.

03 / WEATHER

Weather checks

Wind and gusts can affect drone stability and smooth footage. Rain, visibility, and equipment limits also matter. Conditions are reviewed before takeoff; unsuitable weather means discussing a reschedule. No flight or specific view is guaranteed.

04 / DELIVERY

Editing and delivery

After a confirmed shoot, I’ll edit and deliver the agreed photos or video digitally. Photo resolution and any 4K video delivery must be confirmed for the equipment and format before booking.
